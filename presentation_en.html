Context & Opportunity
"A machinist switching inserts on a live CNC shouldn't have to open a laptop or dig through a catalogue to recalculate feed and speed."
⚠
The Problem
Cutting parameter calculations (vc, n, f, ap, hex, Pc…) are complex, formula-driven and error-prone when done mentally or with scattered reference sheets. Mistakes cost time, tooling and surface quality.
✦
Our Answer
CutCalc Pro puts every SPK CeramTec formula in a single, installable app that works on any smartphone — in the workshop, offline, in seconds.

Technical Architecture
Intentionally simple. Zero dependencies, zero servers, zero maintenance cost.
📦 Single-File PWA
The entire application — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, all formulas, all fonts (Open Sans, DM Mono, embedded as base64 WOFF2) and the SPK CeramTec logo — is contained in one file: index.html.
No build tool. No framework. No CDN dependency at runtime.
🔒 Privacy & Security
All data stays on the device. localStorage is used for preferences and history — no external calls, no analytics, no tracking.
⚙ Service Worker
A Service Worker pre-caches index.html on first visit. Navigation requests are network-first (always fresh when online), falling back to cache when offline. Assets are cache-first.
🌐 Deployment
Served over HTTPS on Surge (static hosting). GitHub repository at . Could be hosted on any static host (, S3, Azure Blob…).
