Getting Started & Installation
ℹ
CutCalc Pro is a Progressive Web App (PWA). No App Store. No download. Open the link in your browser and optionally install it to your home screen for a native-app experience.
Install on iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1
Open Safari and go to cutcalc-spk.surge.sh
2
Tap the Share button (⬆ square with arrow) at the bottom of the screen
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4
Confirm the name CutCalc Pro and tap Add — the SPK logo appears on your home screen
5
The app now launches full-screen, without the Safari address bar. Works offline from this point.
Install on Android (Chrome)
1
Open Chrome and go to cutcalc-spk.surge.sh
2
Chrome may show an "Add to Home Screen" banner automatically — tap it
3
Or tap the ⋮ menu → Add to Home Screen manually
4
Tap Install — the app appears like a native app in your launcher
Offline Functionality
📴
After the first visit, CutCalc Pro works 100% offline. All fonts, formulas and assets are embedded in the app. No internet connection is needed for any calculation.

Performing a Calculation
1
Select a tab (T / M / D) at the bottom of the screen
2
For Turning, pick the sub-operation (EXT · FACE · GROOVE · CUTOFF) in the top segment bar
3
Tap the calculation you want (e.g. "Cutting Speed vc") — it expands showing its input fields
4
Fill in the values — the result updates in real time as you type
5
The result is displayed large in the fixed header area, with the full formula below it

Turning — Formula Reference
Sub-operations: EXT (External) · FACE · GROOVE · CUTOFF
📘
All formulas are sourced from the official SPK by CeramTec catalogue. Values in italic are constants or user-selected parameters (Kc, RE).
Symbol Parameter Formula Unit
vc Cutting speed vc = (π × DC × n) / 1000 m/min
n Spindle speed n = (vc × 1000) / (π × DC) RPM
vf Table feed vf = n × f mm/min
Ra Surface roughness Ra = f² / (8 × RE) μm
Pc Net cutting power Pc = (ap × f × vc × Kc) / (60 × 10³) kW
H Horsepower (SPK) H = Pc / 0.75 HP
MRR Material removal rate MRR = ap × f × vc × 1000 cm³/min

Milling — Formula Reference
Official SPK CeramTec catalogue formulas · Corrected v1.8.1
Symbol Parameter Formula Unit
vc Cutting speed vc = (π × DC × n) / 1000 m/min
n Spindle speed n = (vc × 1000) / (π × DC) RPM
vf Table feed vf = fz × z × n mm/min
fz Feed per tooth fz = vf / (z × n) mm/tooth
hex Chip thickness
(ae ≤ DC/2 — partial engagement)		 hex = 2 × fz × sin(κ) × √(ae/DC − (ae/DC)²) mm
hex Chip thickness
(ae > DC/2 — full/over engagement)		 hex = fz × sin(κ) mm
hex₂ Chip thickness
(round insert)		 hex = 2 × fz × √(ap/IC − (ap/IC)²) mm
ae Radial width of cut — mm
ap Axial depth of cut — mm
Pc Net cutting power Pc = (ae × ap × vf × Kc) / (60 × 10⁶) kW
H Horsepower (SPK) H = Pc / 0.75 HP
MRR Material removal rate MRR = ae × ap × vf / 1000 cm³/min
🤖
Auto chip-thickness formula selection (v1.8.2): the app detects automatically whether ae ≤ DC/2 (partial engagement) or ae > DC/2 (full/over engagement) and applies the correct formula. The formula string updates in real time below the result.

Settings & Preferences
🌍 Language
Toggle between FR (French) and EN (English). All labels, units and the in-app guide switch immediately. Preference saved for next launch.
📏 Unit System
Toggle between mm and inch. All input values are converted live (×÷25.4), all unit labels update. Preference saved automatically.
🌙 Dark Mode
Full dark theme optimised for shop-floor use and OLED screens. Tap the moon icon in the header. Preference saved.
🧱 Material (Kc / HB)
Select material group from the list. Kc and HB values are saved per material selection and auto-restored each time you open a calculation.
History & Favourites
  • The last 5 calculations per operation are stored in-session. Tap the clock 🕐 icon in the result bar to open the history bottom sheet.
  • Tap any history entry to restore all input values exactly as they were.
  • Tap the star ★ on any calculation in the list to mark it as a favourite — starred items float to the top and are persisted across sessions.

Tips & Quick Reference
Power User Tips
  • Partial entry: you don't need to fill all fields — the result updates as soon as enough inputs are present. Missing fields show —.
  • Haptic confirmation: the phone vibrates briefly (30 ms) each time a valid result is computed — useful when you can't look at the screen.
  • PDF calculation sheet: tap 🖨 to get a printable sheet with formula, all inputs, result, notes and date — ideal for documentation or work orders.
  • Share: tap ⬆ to send the full calculation (parameters + result + notes) via SMS, email or copy to clipboard.
  • Notes field: an expandable text area below inputs lets you add part name, material or operation notes — included in the share and print output.
  • ✕ button: clears only the tapped field — all other values stay. Use this to quickly test a different parameter.
  • Reset ↺: clears all fields for the current calculation at once.
  • Swipe navigation (Turning): swipe left/right on the input area to switch between EXT → FACE → GROOVE → CUTOFF.
